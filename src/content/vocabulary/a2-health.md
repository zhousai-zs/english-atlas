## 这一课怎么学

不舒服的基本句是 I feel ill. My head hurts. I have a pain in my back. 建议用 should：You should rest. You should see a doctor. 吃药是 take medicine，不是 eat medicine，虽然中文说“吃药”。

sleep 作名词时不可数或可数都可以：I need sleep. I had a good sleep. 动词的过去式是 slept。
