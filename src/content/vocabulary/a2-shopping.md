## 这一课怎么学

购物对话的骨架是：How much is this? It costs 40 yuan. That is expensive. I will take it. Can I pay by card? 三个“花”要看主语。I spent 40 yuan. The book cost 40 yuan. I paid 40 yuan for the book. spend 的过去式是 spent。

cheap 不要用来形容价格本身说 a cheap price，更自然的是 a low price，或者说 The shirt is cheap.
